# Build a Full Product Specification for a Pakistan-First Field Agent Marketplace

I have an app idea and I want you to act as a senior product architect, marketplace designer, UX designer, backend architect, security engineer, and business-system analyst.

Do NOT simplify the idea into a normal delivery app.

The core concept is:

> A buyer can hire a verified local "Field Agent" who physically visits an online seller on the buyer's behalf, verifies the seller and product, inspects and tests the product, communicates with the buyer through WhatsApp/video call, negotiates if authorized, completes the purchase process, packages the item, and hands it to a courier or personally delivers it when practical.

The purpose is to make remote online purchases safer, especially when the buyer and seller are in different cities.

The platform launches in Pakistan first but should be architected so it can expand internationally later.

---

# 1. CORE PROBLEM

Online buyers frequently find products on platforms such as Facebook Marketplace, OLX, WhatsApp, local marketplaces, classified websites, social media, and other seller platforms.

The buyer may be interested in a product located in another city but cannot physically visit the seller.

The risks include:

- Fake sellers
- Fake locations
- Stolen or counterfeit products
- Product condition being different from photographs
- Defective products
- Missing accessories
- Hidden damage
- Seller refusing to demonstrate the product
- Bait-and-switch
- Incorrect specifications
- Fraudulent payment requests
- Seller disappearing after payment
- Buyer having no trusted person nearby to inspect the item

The Field Agent marketplace solves this by allowing the buyer to hire a verified human in the seller's city.

# 3. FIELD AGENT

Call the local worker a:

**Field Agent**

Do not call them merely a delivery driver.

Their primary role is to act as the buyer's physical representative.

A Field Agent should be able to:

1. Receive available buyer requests
2. See the general task requirements
3. See the seller's city/location
4. See the proposed Field Agent payment/bid
5. Accept a task
6. Navigate to the seller
7. Verify the seller's identity
8. Verify that the seller is actually located at the stated location
9. Confirm that the seller has possession of the product
10. Physically inspect the product
11. Take photographs
12. Record videos
13. Test the product
14. Compare the physical product with the online listing
15. Check serial numbers/IMEI/model numbers where applicable
16. Check accessories and included components
17. Identify visible damage
18. Report discrepancies
19. Video call the buyer through WhatsApp when requested
20. Show the buyer the product live
21. Allow the buyer to ask questions
22. Negotiate with the seller when the buyer has authorized negotiation
23. Report the negotiated price
24. Obtain buyer approval before final purchase
25. Complete the purchase process
26. Collect the product
27. Package the product appropriately
28. Photograph/video the packaged item
29. Hand the package to a courier
30. Personally deliver it to the buyer when the distance/rules allow it
31. Upload proof of handover
32. Complete the job
33. Receive their share of the transaction commission
34. Receive their inspection/task payment

The Field Agent must never secretly alter the buyer's instructions.

# 6. FIELD AGENT BIDDING / HIRING

The buyer chooses the Field Agent.

The buyer can publish a task/request.

Field Agents near the seller's location can see eligible requests.

Depending on the marketplace design, Field Agents can either:

- accept the buyer's offered task payment, or
- submit their own bid.

The system must support a clear bidding/acceptance workflow.

Once a Field Agent accepts the task, the task payment should be secured according to the platform's payment rules.

IMPORTANT:

The Field Agent's task/inspection payment is separate from the 10% transaction commission.

---

# 7. INSPECTION PAYMENT

The buyer must pay the Field Agent for undertaking the job.

This payment exists even if the purchase fails.

Example:

Buyer creates a request.

Field Agent accepts.

Buyer pays the Field Agent task fee.

Field Agent visits seller.

Field Agent discovers that:

- product is fake,
- product is damaged,
- seller lied,
- seller refuses inspection,
- seller doesn't have the product,
- price is unacceptable,
- product doesn't match listing,
- buyer rejects the product.

The purchase is cancelled.

The buyer still pays the agreed Field Agent task/inspection fee because the Field Agent performed the physical service.

The 10% transaction commission is NOT charged because the purchase did not close.

---

# 8. 10% TRANSACTION COMMISSION

The platform charges:

**10% of the final completed purchase value**

ONLY when the transaction successfully closes.

Example:

Product final purchase price:

Rs. 100,000

Transaction commission:

10% = Rs. 10,000

This Rs. 10,000 is split:

- 50% = Rs. 5,000 to the Field Agent
- 50% = Rs. 5,000 to the platform

Therefore:

Field Agent receives:

Task/inspection payment + Rs. 5,000 transaction commission

Platform receives:

Rs. 5,000 transaction commission

If the deal is cancelled:

Transaction commission = Rs. 0

The Field Agent still receives the agreed inspection/task payment.

The system must make this distinction extremely clear in the UI.

# 9. PAYMENT ESCROW / PROTECTED TRANSACTION

The buyer's purchase money should be protected by the platform's transaction system.

Do not simply send the purchase money directly to the seller before verification.

Recommended conceptual flow:

Buyer funds protected transaction.

↓

Field Agent visits seller.

↓

Field Agent verifies and inspects product.

↓

Field Agent communicates findings to buyer.

↓

Buyer approves product.

↓

Buyer authorizes purchase.

↓

Purchase funds are released according to the transaction rules.

↓

Field Agent obtains/collects product.

↓

Product is packaged.

↓

Product is handed to courier.

↓

Courier tracking begins.

The exact legal/payment implementation must be designed according to Pakistan's applicable financial, marketplace, escrow, consumer-protection, and payment regulations.

Do not falsely describe the system as legally regulated escrow unless the required regulatory/payment structure actually exists.

- Damage
- Functionality
- Accessories
- Packaging
- Seller
- Location/context where appropriate

The buyer can ask:

"Turn on the camera."

"Open Settings."

"Show the battery health."

"Test the speaker."

# 11. NEGOTIATION SYSTEM

The buyer can optionally authorize the Field Agent to negotiate.

Example:

Listing price:

Rs. 120,000

Buyer maximum:

Rs. 110,000

Instruction:

"Try to purchase for Rs. 105,000. Do not exceed Rs. 110,000 without asking me."

The Field Agent can negotiate.

The system should support:

- Original asking price
- Negotiated price
- Buyer's maximum authorized price
- Field Agent negotiation permission
- Buyer approval
- Seller counteroffer
- Final agreed price

The Field Agent must not exceed the buyer's authorized maximum without explicit buyer approval.

Examples:

- Seller unavailable
- Seller location incorrect
- Seller refuses inspection
- Seller refuses video call
- Product unavailable
- Product doesn't match listing
- Product defective
- Product counterfeit/suspicious
- Product significantly damaged
- Price exceeds buyer authorization
- Seller changes terms
- Buyer rejects product after inspection
- Safety issue
- Field Agent cannot safely complete the task
- Other documented reason

When a deal fails:

Purchase transaction = cancelled

10% transaction fee = NOT charged

Field Agent task/inspection payment = remains payable according to the agreed task terms.

The system should record evidence and reason for cancellation.

# 25. VERY IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE

"Uber but for shopping."

Delivery is only one part of the workflow.

Inspection + verification + live communication + negotiation + purchase + collection are the core service.
